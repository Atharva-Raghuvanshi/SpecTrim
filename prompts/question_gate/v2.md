# Question Gate Policy v2

Tightened deterministic question selection policy (FR-6):
- Strictly restrict blocking question categories to architectural forks:
  auth_model, data_ownership, payments, target_platform.
- Reject categories like data_model, styling, behavior_detail, library_choice from question queue (routed to assumptions).
- Require minimum confidence >= 0.70 for blocking items to be asked as questions.
- Low-confidence (<0.70) blocking items become suppressed assumptions.
- Capped at max_questions per round (default 3).
- Overflow items become flagged non-overridable assumptions.
- Defaultable items always become overridable assumptions.
